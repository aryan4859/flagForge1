## 📝 About FlagForge

**FlagForge** is a modern Capture The Flag (CTF) platform designed to host, manage, and participate in security challenges.  
It provides an **intuitive interface**, secure flag submission, and real-time progress tracking for participants.  

---

## 🚀 Features

- **User-Friendly Interface** – Clean and responsive UI for participants and admins  
- **Profile & Achievements** – Track progress, completed challenges, and earned badges  
- **Flag Submission** – Secure and efficient flag validation system  
- **Leaderboard** – Real-time scoring to track top performers  
- **Badges & Recognition** – Earn badges for valid vulnerability reports or challenge completion  
- **Stats & Analytics** – Track your performance over time

## 🕹 Usage

### Participant

1. Sign in with Google account  
2. Browse challenges by category or difficulty  
3. Submit flags to earn points and badges  
4. Track your progress and ranking on the leaderboard  

### Admin

- Manage challenges, users, and submissions  
- Monitor platform stats and analytics  
- Award badges and recognition for vulnerability reports
